Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, and STK Royal Yeast.

What is the minimum order quantity? ▾
You can order as little as a single unit at ₦530. A standard carton contains 36 units priced at ₦18,260. We also offer bulk and wholesale pricing for resellers and commercial kitchens — contact us for a custom quote.
Do you offer nationwide delivery in Nigeria? ▾
Yes, we deliver across Nigeria. Delivery timelines and costs vary by location. Contact us via phone or WhatsApp to confirm delivery to your area and get an accurate delivery quote.
Is STK Royal Yeast safe for children? ▾
Absolutely. STK Royal is made from naturally sourced ingredients with no artificial additives, making it completely safe for all ages — including children. It's nutritious, wholesome, and trusted by Nigerian families.
Can I become a distributor for STK Royal? ▾
Yes! We welcome distribution partnerships across Nigeria and internationally. Select "Distribution Partnership" from the subject dropdown in our contact form, or call us directly to discuss terms and minimum order requirements.
How do I pay for my order? ▾
We accept payment via bank transfer, Paystack (debit card / online), Visa, and Mastercard. Details will be provided when you confirm your order via phone or WhatsApp.
What makes STK Royal different from other yeast brands? ▾
STK Royal is distinguished by its 100% natural ingredients, recyclable white and cream packaging, baking-grade quality, and versatility across dishes. It's formulated to deliver consistent, professional results whether you're a home baker or an expert chef.
