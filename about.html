Our Journey

The Story Behind
the Sachet

STK Royal began with a question asked in a professional kitchen: why should bakers settle for yeast that is inconsistent, artificially enhanced, and packaged wastefully? The answer became our company.

We spent years perfecting a formula that honours tradition — yeast as it should be, derived from naturally sourced ingredients, enhanced only by time and expertise.

"Great baking begins with great ingredients. STK Royal is our promise that you will never have to compromise."
— The STK Royal Founding Team

Today, STK Royal is the trusted baking companion for thousands of professional chefs, bakeries, and passionate home cooks across Nigeria and beyond — all united by a love for food made right.
